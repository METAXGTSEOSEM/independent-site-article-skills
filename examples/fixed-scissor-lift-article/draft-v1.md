# Fixed Scissor Lift Platforms: A Buyer's Checklist Before You Request a Quote

Fixed scissor lift platforms are a popular choice for warehouses and maintenance teams that need repeatable access to a work area. They are generally safer than mobile lifts because the platform does not move while it is raised. This guide explains what to check before requesting a quote.

## Start with the work, not the equipment

Before comparing suppliers, describe the task the platform must support. Include the people, tools and materials that will be on the platform, how often the task will be repeated, and whether the work area is fixed.

## Check capacity and height

Choose a platform with high capacity and enough height for the job. Ask the supplier for the load rating and confirm that it covers the intended use. The platform should also fit the available space.

## Review access and installation

Check the approach route, floor condition and overhead clearance. A fixed platform may require preparation work before installation. Ask what the supplier needs from the site.

## Compare controls and safety features

Ask about controls, guarding and emergency lowering. The supplier should explain how the platform is inspected and maintained. Training requirements should also be discussed.
