# How to Choose a Project Management Tool for a Small Agency

Choosing project management software is one of the most important decisions a small agency can make. The most popular tool is usually the safest choice, and the cheapest plan is often enough for a growing team. This guide explains what to compare.

## Start with your projects

List the work your agency handles and how each project moves from brief to delivery. Consider client communication, reviews, approvals and reporting.

## Compare features

Look for tasks, timelines, files, comments and reporting. The best tool will have every feature you need. Ask vendors for a demo and compare the checklists.

## Check the price

Choose a plan that fits your budget. Most teams can start on the lowest tier and upgrade later. Ask about discounts and annual billing.

## Run a trial

Try the tool with your team for a week. If everyone likes it, you can migrate your projects and start using it.
