## Google

- [Documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates): check documentation changes without calling every update an algorithm change.
- [Helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): reader value, original help, and credible authorship.
- [Generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content): verify accuracy and context before publication.
- [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): avoid keyword stuffing and low-value scaled production.
- [AI features and websites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): search eligibility and snippet access without a selection guarantee.
- [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): check current guidance before recommending special files, passage lengths, or AI-specific markup.
- [Search generative AI control](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024): verify current documentation, effective account settings, inheritance, and the distinction from training controls. Do not assume a site's actual configuration.
- [Technical requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/technical): crawling, successful responses, and indexable content do not guarantee indexing.
- [Title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet): accurate wording; writing-length suggestions are not mandatory display limits.
- [Article structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article): apply only where appropriate and check CMS duplication.
- [Requesting recrawling](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl): discovery and troubleshooting do not guarantee indexing.

## Editorial heuristics

Definitions, concise answers, conditions, tables, and steps can help readers. They do not guarantee AI citation.

Word count, density, paragraph length, link quotas, readability scores, GEO scores, NLP scores, and detector scores are not official pass thresholds.

Humanizer edits expression; it does not establish authorship or justify deleting attribution and required disclosure.
